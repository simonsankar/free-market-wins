- _How_ man should conduct himself
- This stems from [[Epistemology]], the theory of knowledge — how we come to know things.
- [[Epistemology]] comes from [[Metaphysics]], which deals with the first principles of things, including abstract concepts like knowing, cause, identity, time, space etc.
- Ethics applied to resolving conflicts between men is [[Law]] — not a separate branch of philosophy. See [[Philosophy]] for the full chain, and for where a popular account of it wrongly adds one.
