**Metaphysics is the study of existence as such — reality, and what it means for anything at all to be.**

## What It Answers

Before anything can be known ([[Epistemology]]) or acted on ([[Ethics]]), there has to be something to know and act within. Metaphysics answers the most basic question there is: what is real, and what is its nature?

## The Three Axioms

Everything else rests on three irreducible, self-evident axioms — each provable only by the fact that denying it requires using it (see [[Axioms as Invulnerable]]):

- **[[Existence]]** — something exists, as opposed to nothing.
- **[[Identity]]** — to exist is to be something specific; A is A.
- **[[Consciousness]]** — the faculty of perceiving that which exists.

## The Fork That Decides Everything Downstream

All of metaphysics reduces to one question: does existence hold primacy over consciousness, or the reverse?

- **[[Primacy of Existence]]** (correct): reality is what it is, independent of anyone's wishes, feelings, or beliefs. Consciousness's job is to perceive and identify reality, not author it.
- **[[Primacy of Consciousness]]** (the fallacy): the idea that wishing, believing, or willing something can make it so. Self-refuting — consciousness presupposes something to be conscious *of*, which is reality, so reality has to come first.

Every downstream error catalogued in this vault — [[Socialism is impossible|central planning]], [[Subjective Value Theory|value subjectivism]] misapplied past economics into ethics, [[Polylogism]], [[Mixed Law]] — is ultimately a Primacy of Consciousness error: some claim that reality (economic law, human nature, the nature of value) bends to a plan, a class, or a vote, instead of the other way around.

## Feeds Into

Metaphysics doesn't stop at "what is." Because reality — including *human* identity, i.e. what kind of entity man is — has a fixed nature, metaphysics is what makes an objective [[Epistemology]] possible, which is what makes an objective [[Ethics]] possible. See [[Philosophy]] for the full chain, and for where a popular account of it (Objectivism) adds a branch that doesn't belong.
