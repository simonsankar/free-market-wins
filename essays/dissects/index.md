Essay-length teardowns of specific government policies, arguments, and public statements — applying Austrian economics and the NAP to real cases rather than abstract theory.

Most entries here are grouped by target: `3rd-world-woes/` covers Trinidad and comparable developing-world government failures. A **developed-world** category is reserved for future dissects of rich-country statism — Europe's welfare states, US redistribution rhetoric, and similar — and will be created once the first piece for it exists. Dissects that don't target a specific place sit ungrouped at this level.
