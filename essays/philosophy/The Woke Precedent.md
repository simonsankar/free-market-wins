title: The Woke Precedent
date: 2026-09-17
---

**The standard objection to ancap society is that it needs a population of saints — people who just happen to hold the line on the NAP with nobody forcing them to. That's treated as the fantasy part. It isn't. We're already watching that exact mechanism run, in public, at scale, aimed at something else.**

## Conviction Without a Badge

Nobody passed a law requiring EA to add women's international teams to a football game nobody asked for. No regulator fines a studio for not hiring a narrative consultancy. No court order made a AAA game gender-swap its cast against the wishes of the audience that was going to pay for it. Every one of these choices was made by people who could have made a different, more profitable one, and didn't, because they believe something and they're building the world according to that belief whether or not the world wants it.

Look at how far this actually strays from reality, because that's the part worth not softening. Women's football isn't remotely close to the men's game — anyone who's played the sport at any real level already knows this without needing a stat, and the stat exists anyway: a boys' under-15 academy side beat the senior U.S. women's national team, the best team in the world at the time, 5–2 in a 2017 practice match. There was nothing here to fix. No petition, no feature request, no gap in the market anyone was pointing at. And yet the games put the best women's players on the exact same 1-to-99 rating scale as prime male professionals, clustered near the top of it — not because the underlying attributes support that placement, but because an honest scale would say something about the gap between the sexes that the people building the rating system have decided is unacceptable to say. That's not an oversight. That's reality being overruled on purpose, because the people doing the overruling are so convicted that the distortion feels less costly to them than the truth would.

It isn't confined to sports games. Nothing about Disney's *Little Mermaid* or *Snow White* was broken in a way that demanded a new Ariel or a rewritten Snow White and her dwarfs — no audience revolt, no lapsed franchise, no commercial hole to fill. The studio rewrote them anyway, and *Snow White* went on to badly underperform at the box office after months of the changes themselves being the story, before a single ticket was sold. GTA has never needed a female lead to justify its own crime-saga formula, and Rockstar built its marketing for *GTA VI* around Lucia specifically, an explicit self-described "Bonnie and Clyde" framing, because that's the story the people making it wanted to tell — not because thirty years of the series' own track record was pointing there. None of these fixed anything. None of them were demanded. Every one of them happened because somebody with the budget and the platform had a conviction, and decided reality — the source material, the box office, the sport itself — was going to answer to the conviction and not the other way around.

Sometimes the market does eventually collect its due. *Concord* — a hero shooter built almost entirely around this ideology's aesthetic priorities — shipped in 2024, sold almost nothing, and was pulled within two weeks, at a loss reported in the hundreds of millions. That's the important data point, not the failure itself: a rational, profit-seeking studio doesn't greenlight that project, staff it, and ship it. A studio full of people who believe something does, and keeps believing it right up until the servers get shut off. The market punished the outcome. It did nothing to stop the conviction that produced it, which is still out there, still hiring, still building the next one.

## The Harder Case Isn't the One Ancaps Need

That's the point worth sitting with. This ideology had to fight the profit motive to get what it wanted, and it won often enough to reorganize the creative output of an entire industry — studios hire for ideological fit as much as talent, consult firms like Sweet Baby Inc. specifically to keep the product "on message," and route around dissenting voices internally the same way any covenant community would screen an applicant at the gate. Nobody's holding a gun to any of these people. They just don't want to work with, sell to, or platform anyone who doesn't share the conviction. That's freedom of association doing exactly what [[private property|property rights]] say it's allowed to do — they just happen to be aiming it at something false.

If uncoerced conviction can survive losing hundreds of millions of dollars and keep reproducing itself through self-selected hiring, gatekeeping, and multi-decade cultural pressure, then the ancap doesn't need a *stronger* version of that mechanism. He needs a *weaker* one — because his conviction isn't fighting the incentive structure, it's riding it. [[Trade|Voluntary exchange]] and [[Sound Money|sound money]] compound the wealth of everyone who practices them consistently. Nobody has to choose between being principled and being prosperous in a NAP-adherent enclave the way *Concord*'s studio had to choose between ideology and the balance sheet. The ideology and the incentive point the same direction. That was always the easier version of this problem.

## The Community Part Isn't Hypothetical Either

The second half of the objection — fine, individuals can believe things, but can a whole *community* run on conviction alone, generation after generation, with no state doing the enforcing? — also doesn't need a hypothetical. Amish, Hutterite, and Hasidic enclaves have been doing it for centuries: privately governed, internally coherent moral and economic codes, transmitted to children as unquestioned common sense rather than imposed doctrine, and enforced not by police but by exclusion — shunning is nothing but private property owners declining to trade with, associate with, or house someone who broke the community's terms. Nobody's conscripting the Amish into the Amish. People who don't want the deal leave, and the ones who stay mostly never seriously consider otherwise, because it's simply what reality looks like to someone raised inside it.

That's the whole mechanism the objection claims doesn't exist, already running, for far longer than any nation-state has run unbroken.

## What an Ancap Enclave Gets That Neither Precedent Had

Put the two together and the ancap case is stronger than either one on its own. The entertainment industry proves conviction alone, with no state behind it, is enough to durably reorganize behavior against financial interest. The enclave communities prove that conviction can organize a whole self-governing society across generations with zero state apparatus, using nothing but [[Contracts|private contract]] and reputation to enforce its own rules. Neither one had to work against as few obstacles as a NAP-adherent [[Anarcho-Capitalism|covenant]] would. The industry has to keep fighting the market to hold its position. The Amish hold their position partly *through* deliberate material austerity — plainness is the point. A community built on honest [[Homesteading (First-comer Ethic)|homesteading]], [[Markets|free exchange]], and sound money doesn't have to choose piety over prosperity. Every generation that holds the line gets richer for having held it, which is the one input neither precedent had working in its favor.

None of this requires converting anyone who doesn't want it. It requires the opposite — the same screening every ideologically captured studio and every enclave already does at the door. A private neighborhood that only re-ups the lease on people who don't steal, don't defraud, and pull their own weight isn't a fantasy of universal buy-in. It's a bouncer, and bouncers already exist.

Take the zero-tolerance instinct at face value instead of softening it, because it's the load-bearing part. A studio doesn't quietly shrug at a writer who wants to tell the old kind of story — the ideology polices its own with real teeth, socially and professionally, the instant someone steps out of line. A NAP-adherent enclave runs on the identical reflex, pointed at aggression instead of authenticity. Your neighbor doesn't shrug when you break a contract or take something that isn't yours. He says *no, that's not what we do here*, with the same certainty and the same lack of patience for debate that the industry brings to enforcing its own line — and everyone else on the block backs him, immediately, because they hold the exact same conviction he does. That's not a weaker community for being intolerant of the wrong things. Intolerance of the one thing that actually matters — aggression against person or property — is the whole mechanism that keeps this from decaying back into the world it opted out of.

## Two, Three Generations In

Run this forward the way the enclaves already have. Kids who grow up trading in sound money and settling disputes through arbitration and reputation don't experience the NAP as a rule they're obeying — it's just what a transaction *is* to them, the same way a Hasidic kid doesn't experience Shabbat as an imposition. Someone explains that most of the world still uses currency that depreciates by design, administered by an institution that can print more of it whenever it wants, and the reaction won't be a policy disagreement. It'll be the same reaction anyone has on first learning a neighboring culture does something they'd never do to their own family: *why would anyone live like that.*

That reaction, multiplied across a couple of generations who never had to be persuaded because they were never taught anything else, is the actual endgame — not because it was forced on anyone, but for the same reason a studio keeps casting the same message film after film after the last one lost the company nine figures. Conviction doesn't need to win the argument every time. It just needs a generation raised inside it who never has to.
