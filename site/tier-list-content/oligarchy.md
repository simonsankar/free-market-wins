The claim: without elections or regulatory oversight, there's nothing to stop capital owners from running the whole society for their own benefit — no state means no check on wealth turning into unaccountable rule.

Look at where oligarchy actually comes from today: not from free exchange, but from regulatory capture, licensing cartels, and subsidies — state power that a handful of connected firms lobby to bend in their favor, at everyone else's expense. That's the opposite of a free market, which holds capital accountable *every single transaction*: a firm that stops serving customers better than competitors loses their business immediately, with no waiting for an election cycle and no need to win a majority vote first — see [[The Confiscation Fantasy — Why Seizing Every Billionaire Wouldn't Help You|why concentrated wealth is mostly illiquid equity tied to ongoing performance]], not a stash of unaccountable power sitting in a vault. Compare that to the "accountability" elections actually provide: a vote every few years among a captive population with no exit option, deciding who gets to wield a monopoly on force over everyone including the losing side — [[Democracy is garbage|that's a weaker check than daily market competition]], not a stronger one. Removing the state doesn't remove the check on capital, it removes capital's main tool for buying an unaccountable check on everyone else.
