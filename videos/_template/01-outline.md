## Hook — beat by beat

The first fifteen seconds decide the video. Scripts that land a specific value
claim before 0:15 average ~52% retention against ~44% for those that don't.
Write these three beats as actual lines, not descriptions of lines.

## Retention targets

- 65–80% still watching at 0:30
- ~35% average view duration is strong for 15–30 minutes of long-form
- Steepest drop is seconds 10–20 — the fix is always earlier in the script,
  never later in the edit
